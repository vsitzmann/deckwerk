# Collaborating with humans

Click **Collaborate**. DeckWerk starts a live editing session, opens the shared presentation, and copies an invite link to your clipboard.

Send that link to your collaborators. They open it in a browser, enter their name, and edit the same presentation with you. Everyone sees each other's slide, cursor, selection, and edits as they happen.

> Screenshot placeholder: Two people editing the same slide with visible presence.

Use comments when an edit needs discussion. Right-click an object and choose **Add comment…**, or use the comment bubble on a slide.

Anyone in the session can **Download** the current presentation or **Present** it. The host can copy the invite again with **Copy Invite Link**.

## On a phone

Open the same link on a phone and you get a version made for it. Turn the
phone on its side: the slides run down the left, the slide is in the middle,
and **Notes** or **Comments** open beside it. Held upright, the slides run
along under the slide and the notes fill the rest of the screen. You cannot
change a slide there, only its speaker notes. You can read and write
comments, and **All slides** lists every comment in the presentation. The
phone can also **Present**, and its **Agent** button copies the same prompt as
the editor's. **⋯ › Open in the editor** switches to the full editor.

## Using a phone as a remote

Anyone who is presenting can give someone in the session the remote. Click
**Remote…** in Speaker View, or move the mouse in the presentation and click
the **Remote…** button that appears in the corner (or press **R**). Then pick
a person, for example your own phone. They are asked first. If they accept,
their screen becomes a remote: current and next slide, notes, timers and big
**Previous** / **Next** buttons. Swiping across the slide works too. Click
**Take back** to end it, or they tap **Leave remote**. Ending the
presentation ends the remote as well.

Click **End collaboration** to save the work, close the session for everyone, and return to the desktop editor.

Only share collaboration links over a network you trust.

To keep a collection of presentations available without leaving the desktop
editor open, see [Running a headless collaboration server](06-headless-server.md).
