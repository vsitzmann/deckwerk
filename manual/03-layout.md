# Layout

Layout is intentionally simple. Drag objects to move them, drag their handles to resize them, and use the alignment controls under **Props** when several objects are selected.

For placing things by number, the ruler button at the left end of the zoom bar (or **Cmd/Ctrl+R** in the desktop app) shows rulers along the top and left of the canvas. They count in slide units, 0 to 1920 across and 0 to 1080 down, and follow the slide as you zoom and scroll. The selection shows as a band on each ruler and the pointer as a moving tick. The rulers stay on or off until you change them.

With nothing selected, **Props → Layout** offers three starting points: **Freeform**, **Title + body**, and **Title slide**. These only place text boxes; the theme controls how they look.
