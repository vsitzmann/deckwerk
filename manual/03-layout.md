# Layout

Layout is intentionally simple. Drag objects to move them, drag their handles to resize them, and use the alignment controls under **Props** when several objects are selected.

Between the slide list and the canvas sits the **object list**: every object on the slide, front-most at the top, each with a small picture of itself, including ones hidden until a build or parked off the slide. Click a row to select its object (Shift or Cmd adds and removes, as on the canvas), and drag rows up or down to change what sits in front. Each drag is one undo step, like **Bring forward** and **Send backward**. The layers button at its top folds it to a narrow strip and back, and it stays the way you left it.

With nothing selected, **Props → Layout** offers three starting points: **Freeform**, **Title + body**, and **Title slide**. These only place text boxes; the theme controls how they look.
