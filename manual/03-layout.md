# Layout

Layout is intentionally simple. Drag objects to move them, drag their handles to resize them, and use the alignment controls under **Props** when several objects are selected.

The layers button at the end of the sidebar's tabs opens the **object list**: every object on the slide, front-most at the top, including ones hidden until a build or parked off the slide. Click a row to select its object (Shift or Cmd adds and removes, as on the canvas), and drag rows up or down to change what sits in front. Each drag is one undo step, like **Bring forward** and **Send backward**. The list stays open or closed the way you left it.

With nothing selected, **Props → Layout** offers three starting points: **Freeform**, **Title + body**, and **Title slide**. These only place text boxes; the theme controls how they look.
